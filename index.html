Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What services do you offer?

We offer comprehensive building facilities maintenance services including electrical, mechanical, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, painting, civil repairs, roof maintenance, and emergency maintenance. All services are delivered by certified professionals.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency services?

Yes, we provide round-the-clock emergency maintenance services. Our dedicated team is available 24/7 to handle any urgent facility maintenance needs to minimize downtime.

How quickly can you respond to maintenance requests?

Our average response time is within 2 hours for emergency requests and 24 hours for scheduled maintenance. We prioritize rapid response to minimize disruption to your operations.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, all our technicians are fully certified, licensed, and insured. We maintain strict quality standards and safety protocols to ensure the highest level of service.

Can you work within our budget constraints?

Absolutely. We offer flexible pricing options and can customize service packages to fit your budget. We'll provide detailed quotations with no hidden charges.

Do you provide maintenance contracts?

Yes, we offer comprehensive preventive maintenance contracts tailored to your facility's specific needs. These contracts help extend equipment life and reduce overall maintenance costs.
